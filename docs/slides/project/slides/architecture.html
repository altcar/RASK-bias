Offline, Python scripts scan the data, audit the original model, fit the LEACE eraser and export a single ONNX file with two outputs: the original embedding and the debiased one. Online, the Streamlit app reads a CV PDF or text and a job description, builds six what-if twins of the CV (three regions times two genders), runs everything through ONNX Runtime and shows the original and debiased decisions side by side, flagging bias when the decision changes. Across the dataset, location flips fell from 15.3 to 10.4 percent with matching quality unchanged; the remaining gap is non-linear and is our next step.
